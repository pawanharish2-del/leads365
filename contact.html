Clear Answers to Common Questions

Frequently Asked Inquiries Before Scheduling a Demo

Everything you need to know about system compatibility, training schedules, data migration, and contract terms.

How fast can our sales team start using Leads365 after the demo?
Most small to mid-sized sales teams (5 to 25 telecallers) go live within 24 to 48 hours. Once you confirm your plan, our technical specialists configure your lead capture integrations (Facebook Lead Ads, Google Ads, website webhooks, IndiaMART) within 2 to 4 hours. Historical data import and telecaller training are completed the following morning.
Do we need specialized telecalling hardware or expensive SIP dialers?
No. Leads365 works effortlessly on standard Android and iOS smartphones using your telecallers' existing SIM cards. Callers can also work from their office desktop or laptop using standard Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge web browsers. There are no expensive PBX boxes, PRI lines, or complex VoIP telecom setups required.
Can we visit the Leads365 office in Jaipur for an in-person demonstration?
Yes, absolutely! We warmly welcome business owners, founders, and sales directors to visit our headquarters at Bhamashah Techno Hub, Sansthan Path, Jhalana Gram, Malviya Nagar, Jaipur, Rajasthan 302017. Please call or WhatsApp us on +91 77919 10007 ahead of time so our product leadership can reserve a conference room and prepare custom workflows for your industry.
How does Leads365 protect our customer database from telecaller theft?
Data security is built into the architecture. Telecallers only see the leads specifically assigned to them. You can enable Phone Number Masking (preventing callers from writing down customer mobile numbers), disable CSV/Excel export permissions for non-admin accounts, and monitor all login sessions with IP logs and automated anomaly alerts. Read our full data protections in our Data Security Policy.
What is the pricing model, and are there hidden setup or onboarding charges?
We believe in 100% transparent pricing with zero surprise charges. All standard integrations (Meta Ads, Google Ads, IndiaMART webhooks), unlimited cloud storage, mobile app access, and live onboarding training are included in our published plans. Explore our full breakdown on our Transparent Pricing Page.

Yes! While our engineering headquarters is in Jaipur, Leads365 powers sales teams across major commercial hubs in India including Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, Ahmedabad, Surat, Lucknow, and Kolkata. Explore our localized solutions in our Locations We Serve section.
