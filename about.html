Our Story & Purpose

Built to Eliminate the 50% Lead Leakage Problem

Every single month, ambitious businesses across India invest lakhs of rupees into digital marketing, Meta Ads, Google campaigns, IndiaMART, and Justdial portals. Yet, when inquiries roll in, they get dumped into chaotic spreadsheets or scattered across personal WhatsApp chats.

By the time a tele-caller dials the number or an executive remembers to follow up, the prospect has already bought from a faster competitor. Meanwhile, traditional Western CRMs were too complicated, expensive, and heavy for everyday sales reps to use.

Backed by the technical excellence of Chonexa Technologies in Jaipur, Leads365 was built to solve this exact bottleneck: a lightning-fast, mobile-first sales engine tailored for Indian business communication, automated follow-ups, and 365 days of relentless sales growth.
