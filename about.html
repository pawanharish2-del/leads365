The Origin of Leads365

Built to Eliminate the 50% Lead Leakage Plague in Indian Sales

Every single month, ambitious business owners and marketing teams across India invest lakhs of rupees into paid campaigns—running Meta Ads on Facebook and Instagram, search campaigns on Google PPC, and premium directory subscriptions on IndiaMART, TradeIndia, Justdial, and 99acres. Yet, within 72 hours of launching these campaigns, an alarming reality sets in: over 50% of inbound buyer inquiries vanish into thin air without a single meaningful conversation.

Why does this happen? Because in real-world Indian sales offices, leads get exported once a day into chaotic Excel spreadsheets, forwarded to shared WhatsApp groups, or scribbled onto telecaller desk notepads. Telecallers cherry-pick the freshest inquiries, postpone lukewarm prospects, and forget callbacks. By the time a sales executive finally dials the number 6 to 12 hours later, the prospective customer has already received a quote and booked a visit with a faster, more responsive competitor.

When business founders searched for solutions, they faced an impossible dilemma. Traditional Western enterprise CRMs were exorbitantly expensive (often charging $80 to $150 per seat per month in US Dollars), required months of costly implementation consulting, and featured bloated, intimidating menus that everyday Indian telecallers refused to open.

Headquartered in Rajasthan's premier technology startup hub at Bhamashah Techno Hub, Jaipur, Chonexa Technologies engineered Leads365 (leads365.in) to solve this exact crisis: a lightning-fast, mobile-first sales automation CRM built specifically for Indian telecalling operations, instant WhatsApp communication, and relentless 365-day pipeline growth.

Behind the Code

Our Four Product Engineering Rules

Every line of code and user interface component inside Leads365 is evaluated against four strict operational standards before reaching production.

Rule #1: Sub-3-Second Ingestion Latency

Harvard Business Review research proves that contacting an inbound lead within 5 minutes yields a 21x higher chance of qualifying the prospect compared to waiting 30 minutes. Our webhook microservices ingest leads from Meta, Google, and IndiaMART, trigger duplicate checks, and buzz your telecaller's phone in under 3 seconds.

Rule #2: The One-Thumb Mobile Dialing Workflow

Telecallers don't sit still at desks all day; they dial from commuting cabs, factory floors, and warehouse reception areas. Leads365 is architected for single-thumb ergonomics: tap to dial SIM card, tap to send approved WhatsApp brochure, tap to set callback calendar notification.

Rule #3: Total Data Sovereignty within India

All customer records, telecaller call logs, and WhatsApp interaction histories are securely hosted within Indian cloud data centers (AWS Mumbai Region ap-south-1). We strictly comply with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 regulations and never transfer databases outside national borders.

Rule #4: Management Accountability Through Real Telemetry

Sales managers don't need fabricated manual reports where callers claim they dialed 100 people. Leads365 logs actual call durations, talk-time seconds, timestamped call recordings, and unanswered call rates automatically from the device SIM card.
